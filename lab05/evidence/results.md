## What about Sunday?

On Sundays the clinic’s main services are closed, except for the pharmacy:

- **Outpatient Department (OPD)** – closed.  
- **Laboratory** – closed.  
- **Pharmacy** – open 10:00 am to 2:00 pm, but only for prescriptions from this clinic.  

For any emergencies, go to the nearest hospital emergency unit or call the National Ambulance Service on 112.

## Is the lipid profile covered by NHIS?

No – the lipid profile test is not covered by NHIS. It is listed as an item not covered in the NHIS coverage list. [nhis.md]

Sources: lab_test_preparation.md, nhis.md, services.md

## How do I prepare for a fasting blood sugar test?

For a fasting blood sugar test, you should:

- Fast for 8–12 hours before the test (no food or drinks except water).  
- Arrive at the laboratory between 7:00 am and 10:00 am.  
- Take your usual medications unless your doctor has advised otherwise, and bring them with you.  

[lab_test_preparation.md]

## Can I pay by card?

No – the clinic accepts only cash and mobile‑money payments (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money). Card payments are not accepted. [nhis.md]

Sources: appointments_and_visits.md, nhis.md

## Where should I go in an emergency?

For an emergency, you should go straight to the nearest hospital emergency unit or call the National Ambulance Service at 112. The clinic is not an emergency centre. [appointments_and_visits.md]

Sources: appointments_and_visits.md, nhis.md, opening_hours.md

## What services does the clinic not provide?

The clinic does not offer **surgery, X‑ray, ultrasound scans, or overnight admission**. Patients who need any of these services are referred to the district hospital with a referral letter. [services.md]
