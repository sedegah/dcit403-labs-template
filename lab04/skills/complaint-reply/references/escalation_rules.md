# Escalation rules

Escalate to the clinic/manager (do not send a direct reply) when:
- The customer threatens legal action.
- The complaint is the third or more on the same issue.
- There is a data or privacy concern.
- The issue involves a safety risk (outage affecting a hospital, etc.).
- The customer asks for compensation above GHS 500.

For escalated cases, acknowledge receipt and state that a manager will respond within two working days.
