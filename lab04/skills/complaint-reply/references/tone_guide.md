# Tone guide for complaint replies

- Open with "Dear [name or 'valued customer'],".
- Acknowledge first, explain second, promise last.
- Use "we" not "I" for company actions.
- Avoid "unfortunately" more than once per reply.
- Avoid technical jargon; explain in plain language.
- Keep sentences under 25 words.
- End with "Kind regards, NetLink Ghana Customer Care".
- Never use exclamation marks in billing replies.
