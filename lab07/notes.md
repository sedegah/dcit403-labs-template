# Lab 7 Notes: FIPA-ACL compared with A2A (five lines)

FIPA-ACL messages carry a performative, sender, receiver and content, much like the messages in evidence/messages.jsonl.
A2A replaces the central directory facilitator with an agent card published at a well-known URL.
FIPA-ACL relies on a shared ontology and a facilitator for discovery; A2A relies on HTTP and JSON.
Both define how agents ask, propose, refuse and inform, but A2A is web-native.
In practice A2A agent cards are the modern equivalent of FIPA service descriptions.
