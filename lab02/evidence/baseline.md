## Observation

but it was performed in prose, so nothing checks the calculation; a single
slipped digit would still look convincing. The capital question is worse: the
model answered Kumasi with high confidence, even though Kumasi is in the
Ashanti Region and the capital of Bono East is Techiman. The Makola answer
(no single founder) is plausible but unverifiable from the model alone. A
calculator removes the arithmetic risk and Wikipedia gives a source each
factual claim can be checked against, which is exactly what the ReAct agent
adds in the traces.
